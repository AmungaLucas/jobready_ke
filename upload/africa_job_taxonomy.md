## 8. Knowledge Graph Structure

```
Candidate
  |-- career_family_code (e.g., ENG)
  |-- specialization_codes (e.g., [ENG-MEC, ENG-QUA])
  |-- job_title_ids (e.g., [1, 45, 89])
  |-- skill_ids + proficiency (e.g., {23: 'expert', 45: 'advanced'})
  |-- competency_ids + level (e.g., {12: 'proficient'})
  |-- industry_ids (e.g., [5, 12, 45])
  |-- certification_ids (e.g., [3, 15])
  |-- education (free text, normalized by LLM)
  |
  v
Matching Engine (structured comparison per dimension)
  |
  ^
  |
Job
  |-- career_family_code
  |-- specialization_codes
  |-- required_skill_ids + level
  |-- required_competency_ids + level
  |-- industry_ids
  |-- required_certification_ids
  |-- education_requirements
```

## 9. LLM Normalization Role

The LLM's role is to **normalize unstructured CVs and job descriptions** into this common structure. The matching engine then operates entirely on **structured data**, making it:

- **Fast** - direct ID/code comparisons
- **Explainable** - show which dimensions matched
- **Inexpensive** - no LLM needed at match time
- **Accurate** - multi-dimensional comparison vs title-only
